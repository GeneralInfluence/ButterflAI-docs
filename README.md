# ButterflAI

**A personal social assistant. It does the logistics of friendship so you can do the friendship.**

People aren't bad friends because they don't care — life gets busy, and keeping up takes work: finding a time, picking a
place, remembering who's in, who's bringing what, what changed. ButterflAI takes that work. You tell it what you'd like to
do and with whom, in your own words; it makes the plan, keeps everyone's ButterflAI up to date, and only asks you what only
you can answer.

> ButterflAI is in early testing with friends. This repository describes what it is, how it works and where it's going.
> The code is private.

---

## What it does today

- **Plans from a sentence.** "Dinner with Jane and Sam Friday evening, maybe at that new Thai place" becomes a plan with a
  when, a where and who's in — each detail kept with the words it came from.
- **Plans everyone shares.** Everyone in a plan sees the same thing: when, where, who, a one-line summary, what's still
  being decided, and who has seen the latest change.
- **Creating — what needs doing.** The steps to make a plan happen (book the table, bring the wine, the shopping list with
  what's on it), who's on each, and what's still open.
- **ButterflAIs talk to each other, not to your friends.** Your ButterflAI coordinates with your friends' ButterflAIs; each
  person only ever hears from their own ButterflAI, in its own words.
- **Explore.** Ideas for things to do near you — real events, ways to see friends you haven't seen in a while — that you
  can save and turn into a plan in one tap.
- **Your friends who aren't on ButterflAI hear from you.** For anyone not on ButterflAI, it writes the invite in your voice
  and you send it from your own phone.

## How to use it

Open **[butterflai.social](https://butterflai.social)** on your phone, sign in with your number, and add it to your home
screen. Then just talk to it. See **[docs/using-butterflai.md](docs/using-butterflai.md)**.
