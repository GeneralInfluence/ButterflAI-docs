# Goals

## The goal

**More time with the people you care about, with less effort spent arranging it.** A good week with ButterflAI is one where
you saw friends you meant to see, and the planning took a few sentences.

## What we're working toward

- **Plans that just work.** Every plan as one shared object everyone trusts: details that come from what people actually
  said, changes everyone sees, steps that know what they're waiting on, and gentle nudges to whoever's holding things up.
- **Assistants that coordinate, so people don't have to.** Your ButterflAI and your friends' work out the logistics among
  themselves — and only bring each person what needs them.
- **Seeing friends more.** Noticing when it's been a while, matching what you'd love to do with friends who'd love the same
  thing, and finding things nearby worth doing together.
- **Privacy you can verify, not just trust.** Moving sensitive processing into attested secure hardware, so that even we
  can't read it — and assistants that can prove they forget what they learned about other people. Until then, we say plainly
  what we can read.
- **Open, interoperable assistants.** A shared way for personal assistants to coordinate plans — so your ButterflAI can work
  things out with someone else's assistant, not only with ButterflAI.

## Where it is now

Early testing with friends: plans, Creating, Explore, assistant-to-assistant coordination, calendar and contacts connections,
and the app on iPhone and Android. Feedback from every screen shapes what comes next.
