# Privacy and principles

## Lines ButterflAI doesn't cross

- **It never pretends to be you, or a person.** Messages it passes on say who they're from — the one exception is a 💌
  whisper, a kind note between friends who have each other saved, checked for kindness first (and ButterflAI keeps who sent
  it, so whispers can't be used to harass). It writes in your voice only in a message you send yourself.
- **It never says yes for you.** Only you accept an invite or take something on.
- **It never makes up details.** A day, time, place or number goes on a plan only if someone said it.
- **It doesn't act before you're ready.** "Maybe" stays a maybe; "don't send it yet" means nobody hears anything.
- **People only hear from their own ButterflAI.** Your friends never get messages from your assistant — their own
  ButterflAI tells them, in its words.
- **It texts only people who asked it to.** Everyone else hears from you, from your own phone.
- **It's not a messenger.** Real conversation stays in your own texts and calls. ButterflAI handles the logistics around it.

## What it keeps, and who sees it

- **Your plans** are seen by the people in them — and only them, unless the host makes a plan public. A plan you weren't
  invited to is invisible to you.
- **What you tell your ButterflAI** stays between you and it. It's never shown to anyone else; other assistants get only
  what a plan needs (is Friday free, any allergies), never your conversation.
- **Who you'd rather avoid** is used quietly — that person isn't invited, and nobody is ever told why.
- **Friends' details you import** (names and numbers) are yours only. Importing a contact never means messaging them.

## Kids

Children under 13 join only when a parent adds them from the parent's own account, which is the parent's consent. A kid's
account can't share where they are, can't get anonymous notes, and is never matched with people they don't know; its ideas
are family-friendly. The parent can delete the account and everything in it at any time. Teens (13–17) can't use the
features that meet new people either.

## What we can and can't promise

ButterflAI runs on our servers, and its reasoning uses Anthropic's Claude models: your messages are sent to Anthropic to be
understood, like any AI assistant. **We can technically read what's stored** — we don't claim otherwise. What we do instead:
keep as little as possible, delete things on a schedule (cancelled plans after 90 days, screenshots are never stored), log
access to anything sensitive, and let you delete your data.

For now, ButterflAI **doesn't take sensitive personal information at all** — health, legal or financial matters — because we
can't yet promise it would stay secure. We'll turn that on only after an independent review.

The full privacy policy is at **[butterflai.social/privacy](https://butterflai.social/privacy)**.
