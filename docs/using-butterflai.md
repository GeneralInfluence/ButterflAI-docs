# Using ButterflAI

## Getting started

1. Open **[butterflai.social](https://butterflai.social)** on your phone and sign in with your phone number or your email (you'll get a
   one-time code). If you'd like texts from ButterflAI when you're not in the app, tick the box — it's optional.
   Kids under 13 can't sign up on their own: a parent adds them in Settings → Family, and they sign in with their email.
2. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone; Install on Android) and turn on notifications.
3. Tell it your name, and connect your contacts and calendar if you'd like it to know who your friends are and when you're
   free (Google or iCloud, from Settings).

## The screens

The bar at the bottom has four places:

- **Home** — what needs you now, what you're making happen, and what's coming up.
- **Explore** — ideas near you. Search it, save what you're into (💜 Saved), make any idea a plan.
- **Plans** — your plans as pills along the top (All, then one per plan). For each plan:
  - **myButterflAI** — talk to your ButterflAI about this plan.
  - **Creating** — the steps, who's on them, and lists like the shopping list.
  - **Details** — the plan itself: where it stands, when, where, who, and what's changed.
- **Settings** — your name, connections, notifications, texts, people and groups.

## Talking to it

Just say it the way you'd say it to a friend:

- "Dinner with Jane and Sam Friday evening" — makes a plan and invites them.
- "Put it on the books for tonight, maybe" — a possible plan; nobody is asked anything.
- "Don't send the invites yet" — people are kept on the plan as not invited yet, until you say so.
- "I'm in", "can't make it", "I'll bring the wine" — your answer, or a step you're taking on.
- "What are my friends up to this weekend?" — only what they've chosen to share with you.

You can attach a **screenshot** (📷) — a group chat, a flyer, an event page — or a **file** (📎) such as a shopping list or a
recipe, and it works from what's in it.

## Inviting people who aren't on ButterflAI

ButterflAI writes the invite and puts a **📱 Text them yourself** card in your chat. Tap **Open Messages** and it opens your
texting app with the message filled in — change anything, then send. They answer you directly.

## Changing your mind

- Tap the **✕** in a plan's pill (or swipe a plan left) to delete it, archive it, or say you're not going.
- Under what you said, ButterflAI shows what it did — tap **Undo** if it got something wrong.
- On a plan's Details, **More** shows recent changes; hosts can undo the last one.

## Ideas, bugs and questions

Tap the 🐛 on the right edge of any screen: it captures what you're looking at (you can leave the screenshot out) and asks
what you'd like changed. Every report becomes a fix and a test.
